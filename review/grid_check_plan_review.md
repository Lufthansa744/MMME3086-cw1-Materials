# Review of the additional S–A grid-dependence check

The main agent's proposed independent verification after the five prescribed cases and initial enhanced-RSM attempt is appropriate: nominal global/foil sizes 10/2 mm, 7.071/1.414 mm and 5/1 mm, with growth 1.05. These are analyst-selected refinements, not extra assignment-specified values. Preserve the original Case 1 and all prescribed meshes/results.

Use the same saved CAD, S–A model, physical and turbulence inputs, wall treatment, SIMPLE, discretization, URFs, force reference/directions, sampling stations and initialization policy across all three levels. The original Case 1 uses second-order momentum/pressure but first-order modified viscosity, so do not advertise the study as uniformly second order. Only mesh controls should distinguish the intended spatial comparison.

Record actual cells, nodes, quality, near-wall distribution, normalization, force histories and stopping status on each grid. Report effective two-dimensional h=sqrt(fluid area/cell count) and actual inter-grid ratios; automatic triangle meshes need not scale exactly with nominal edge-size ratios or remain geometrically similar. Refinement also changes the straight-segment approximation of the curved foil boundary and wall distances; acknowledge this contribution.

Compare at least CL/CD and selected pressure/wake features, using consistent locations and data handling. Report adjacent-grid relative changes and whether the progression is monotonic. Measure terminal iterative drift as a separate uncertainty: original Case 1's last-25 force ranges are approximately 0.041 percent drag and 0.017 percent lift, while last-100 ranges are about 0.47/0.45 percent. If an inter-grid change is comparable to the remaining iteration drift, do not classify it as resolved spatial error. Extend or more tightly converge an independent three-grid verification group consistently if needed, retaining the original Case 1 report result.

Only consider Richardson/GCI estimates after checking observed behavior and asymptotic-range assumptions. Three levels help estimate observed order and assess the asymptotic range, as described by the [NASA NPARC spatial convergence tutorial](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/spatconv.html). Do not insert a formal second-order value by assumption, force a GCI through oscillatory/nonasymptotic behavior or call a mesh independent solely because one force difference is small. If those assumptions fail, the actual sensitivity table and an honest limitation remain useful evidence.

The study concerns spatial sensitivity under this model and boundary setup. It cannot remove S–A model-form, transition, inlet-turbulence or confinement errors, and cannot by itself establish experimental validation.
